# Ad-Hoc-SQL-Analysis-to-Provide-Insights-to-Management
AtliQ Hardware, a leading Indian computer hardware company with a growing global presence, aims to strengthen its data analytics team. To identify skilled junior analysts with strong technical and communication abilities, the Data Analytics Director launched a SQL challenge to assess their capabilities.

## 🌟 Problem Statement

**AtliQ Hardware** is a leading computer hardware manufacturer operating across India and other markets. However, the company faces challenges in making timely, data-driven business decisions.

To strengthen its analytics team, **Tony Sharma, Head of Data Analytics**, has initiated a SQL challenge to identify candidates who demonstrate strong technical knowledge, analytical thinking, and business understanding.

### 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used

* **MySQL:** Joins, CTEs, Subqueries, Window Functions, String Functions, Numeric Functions, Date Functions, and more.
* **Power BI:** DAX and interactive visualizations including Bar Charts, Matrix, Line Charts, Maps, Donut Charts, Sunburst Charts, etc.

## 🌟 Key Findings & Solutions

### 📊 Key Findings

The analysis focused on important business metrics, including:

* Gross Profit
* Contribution %
* Overall Sales Performance
* Average Gross Sales
* Average Manufacturing Cost
* Product and customer performance

### 💡 Business Solutions

Based on the analysis, several strategies were identified to improve sales and customer engagement:

* Introduce membership programs to encourage repeat purchases.
* Offer gift certificates and seasonal promotions.
* Create bundled product deals to increase average order value.
* Provide targeted discounts, such as student offers.
* Use data-driven insights to optimize production and sales strategies.

The project demonstrates how **SQL, Power BI, and business analytics** can be combined to transform raw data into actionable insights and support better decision-making.
